Tony Stark is back — and this time the battle isn’t just against enemies, it’s against himself.

After the Battle of New York, Tony’s haunted by anxiety and panic attacks. When a terrifying new “Mandarin” starts a string of bombings, Tony pours everything into building smarter armor and protecting the people he loves. But when his Malibu home is destroyed, he’s left stranded in Tennessee, stripped of most of his suits — and forced to team up with an unlikely ally: a resourceful kid named Harley Keener.

What follows is a high-stakes race to uncover Extremis — a dangerous biotech that can regenerate tissue… and turn people into walking bombs. The villain isn’t who you think (the Mandarin is a front) and the real mastermind, Aldrich Killian, will push Tony to the brink. With Pepper captured and injected with Extremis, Tony rallies a spectacular, crowd-pleasing rescue — calling in dozens of prototype suits with the unforgettable “House Party Protocol.”

Iron Man 3 is equal parts explosive action, surprising twists, and emotional payoff: Tony confronts his fears, chooses the life he wants, and finds peace beyond the armor.

****JOB POSTING****
Job Title: Lead Actor — Tony Stark (Principal Role)
Project: Feature Film (Action / Drama / Thriller) — based on the provided Iron Man 3 summary
Location: Principal photography in multiple locations (Southern California, Tennessee, soundstages)
Contract: Lead principal, 12–16 week shoot + ADR/press; SAG-AFTRA or negotiable
Compensation: Competitive industry rate (negotiable with experience/box-office draw)

Role Summary
We are casting a charismatic, deeply layered actor to portray Tony Stark — a genius billionaire inventor and reluctant hero struggling with anxiety and the aftermath of a major public battle. This role requires star-level presence, razor-sharp wit, and the ability to convey vulnerability and emotional complexity beneath a flamboyant public exterior. The character must carry big action sequences, intimate emotional beats, and comic moments with equal credibility.

Key Responsibilities
- Bring to life a high-profile protagonist who oscillates between cocky showman and haunted, introspective man.
- Deliver emotionally authentic scenes that explore PTSD, panic attacks, and recovery.
- Execute action sequences (including stunt work, wirework, motion-capture suit interactions) safely and convincingly.
- Provide natural, believable chemistry with co-leads (particularly the actress playing Pepper Potts and a young actor playing Harley Keener).
- Collaborate with director, stunt coordinators, VFX and production teams on performance capture and green-screen work.
- Participate in promotional activities (press junkets, premieres) as required.

Required Skills & Experience
- Proven lead film experience in action, drama, or action-comedy films; preference for actors with demonstrated box-office or critical success.
- Exceptional range: comic timing, improvisational skill, and dramatic intensity.
- Ability to portray post-traumatic stress and panic attacks in a sensitive, realistic manner.
- Comfortable with heavy stunt work and motion-capture/suit interaction. Willingness to train extensively for physical scenes.
- Strong voice control and ability to perform dialogue that blends technical jargon with emotional vulnerability.
- Age range: approximately 35–55 (or able to play that range convincingly).
- Fluent in American English (natural or convincing accent).
- Eligible to work on the production’s filming locations; valid travel documentation as required.

Preferred / Desirable
- Previous experience playing complex, charismatic antiheroes or larger-than-life inventors/leaders.
- Experience working with young co-stars and in scenes requiring fast emotional shifts.
- Musicality or precise timing for comedic beats is a plus.
- Ability to contribute to high-profile press engagements and interviews.

Character Traits & Casting Notes
- Magnetic, commanding presence; first impression should be unforgettable.
- Quick-witted and sharp-tongued but with a deeply guarded emotional core.
- Physically fit and agile enough for repeated action sequences; however, the role centers on performance more than superhero physique alone.
- Must convincingly evolve across the film — from anxious, obsessive overwork to reconciliation and peace.

Audition Materials & Instructions
Please submit the following via casting portal/email:
- Current headshot and full-body photo
- Actor resume (highlighting lead roles, stunts/training, awards)
- Showreel (max 5 minutes) focusing on dramatic range, comedic timing, and action material if available
- Short self-tape (see prompts below) — max 3 minutes total
- Contact info and agent representation (if applicable)

Self-tape prompts (select one or more, 60–90 seconds each)
1) Public Face — Deliver a charismatic, press-conference style line that masks inner turmoil. Show charm and underlying tension.
2) Vulnerability — A quiet scene in which you experience a panic attack or relive a traumatic memory; convey fear, desperation, and fragility without melodrama.
3) Heartfelt — Reassure a loved one (Pepper) about your priorities and future; transition from defensiveness to sincerity.
4) Improvised Mentor — A warm, playful exchange with a resourceful young boy (Harley) in which you reluctantly offer help and reveal a softer side.

Callbacks will include chemistry reads (Pepper Potts role) and action movement assessment with stunt coordinators. Selected actors may be asked to perform a short motion-capture/dynamic scene.
